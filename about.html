About Dragonfly Drone Lab

Dragonfly Drone Lab is a Boulder, Colorado-based commercial drone operation offering real estate photography, construction site documentation, property inspection, and marketing content across the Front Range.

Behind the controls is a photographer's eye 40 years in the making — I picked up my first camera at 16, and that same instinct for light and composition now guides every flight.

A 20+ year background in cybersecurity rounds things out, meaning your data is handled with real care from flight to final delivery — not just a beautifully shot image, but a professionally managed one.

The Aircraft

People hear "drone" and picture something loud and huge circling overhead. The DJI Air 3S fits in one hand, folds down to a hefty-sized water bottle, and weighs just over 1.5 pounds.

  • Weight1.6 lb (724 g)
  • Folded size8.43 × 3.98 × 3.5 in (214 × 101 × 89 mm)
  • Unfolded size10.47 × 12.8 × 4.17 in (266 × 325 × 106 mm)
  • Max flight time45 min
  • Max operational altitude400ft above ground level
  • Operating temp14° to 104°F (-10° to 40°C)

Beyond Piloting

Beyond piloting, the shop also builds, repairs, and customizes drones, and offers drone cybersecurity services — helping fleet operators identify vulnerabilities and protect their aircraft, data links, and ground control systems from emerging threats.
